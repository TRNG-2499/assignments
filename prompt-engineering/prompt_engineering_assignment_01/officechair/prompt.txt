--------------
--  Prompt  --
--------------

Write a product description for an ergonomic office chair.

It is intended to be sold to older patients with joint pain, so it must feature minimal movement required
to adjust your position while sitting, possibly with automatic adjustment of the armrests, etc.
Also, it must have wheels on the bottom for ease of moving the chair itself.

Limit your response to about 20 words per feature description, and follow the overall format provided below,
with your text replacing anything in brackets. Describe exactly three features with this format.

-------
<TITLE>
-------

<OVERVIEW>
<A summary of all three features in one or two short sentences. Keep it brief, and do not mention the target audience here.>


<Feature 1>
<short description of feature 1. 2-3 sentences, 20 words roughly>


<SUMMARY>
<ensure that the target audience is mentioned in this last sentence about the product>.


As an example:


-------------------------------
ERGO-JOINT WHEELED OFFICE CHAIR
-------------------------------

An office chair designed for minimal required movement to change positions, including wheels for large adjustments.


MINIMAL MOVEMENT

Designed to reduce movement needed to change your focus and position,
including automatically-adjusting armrests and other ergonomic features.


POWERED WHEELS

Small motor in the base of the chair to avoid using your legs to move. Omnidirectional and supports up to 300 pounds.


COMFORT

Comes with semi-rigid memory foam pads for seating and back support.


ERGO-JOINT is perfect for those who have trouble moving.

--------------
-- Response --
--------------

ERGO-EASE ERGONOMIC OFFICE CHAIR

An ergonomic office chair designed to minimize physical effort when adjusting sitting positions, with automated comfort features and smooth-rolling wheels.

AUTOMATIC POSITION ADJUSTMENT

Intelligent ergonomic controls automatically adjust the backrest, seat angle, and armrests, allowing users to change positions without unnecessary reaching or stretching.

EFFORTLESS MOBILITY

Smooth-rolling, omnidirectional wheels allow the chair to move easily across floors, reducing the physical effort required to reposition it.

ADAPTIVE JOINT SUPPORT

Contoured memory foam cushioning and adjustable lumbar support provide consistent comfort, reducing pressure on sensitive joints during extended periods of sitting.

ERGO-EASE is designed to provide greater comfort, independence, and ease of use for older adults experiencing joint pain or limited mobility.

--------------
-- Analysis --
--------------

Correct tone and almost correct format (missing the dashes and spacing between sentences).
Left out some details regarding the motorized wheels at the bottom, but otherwise feature-complete.

Combining many prompting techniques proves to provide a more desirable result.
