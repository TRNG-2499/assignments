Analysis

Techniques:

Role: Water V2, headphones, shoes
Context: Water V2, headphones, shoes
Constraints: Water V3, headphones
Few-shot: Shoes
Output format: Water V3

Effects:

Water V1 (no guidance): Generic, with invented specs (insulation times, sizes, warranty).
Water V2 (role + context): Punchier tone and focused on my four features, with fewer fabricated details. Features repeated between body and bullets.
Water V3 (constraints + format): Matched the structure and conversational tone, but invented details again since I gave no features.
Headphones (role, context, constraints): Best adherence. Structure and features matched, with little invention.
Shoes (few-shot, context, role): Copied the example’s structure and style, but invented extra features and ran long.

Ignored or invented
The model made up details whenever I omitted features (Water V1, Water V3) and added extras in the shoe prompt. Fixes: supply real features and add “only describe the features listed.” Also, implementing a length requirement could be helpful for shortening or lengthening responses.

Biggest difference
Context. It determined whether the content was accurate or invented. Few-shot most affected style, but it worked best on top of good context.

Least Difference
Role prompting. In this particular situation changing the role did not affect the output style or quality as much as other techniques.
