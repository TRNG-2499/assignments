## Design Rationale (Product Notes)

**Audience:** Endurance and team-sport athletes, CrossFit and gym regulars, and coaches/trainers who need reliable hydration through long training blocks and travel.

**Core insight:** Athletes lose performance to heat, cold and dehydration. A bottle that holds temperature for days means less refilling, safer hydration in extreme conditions, and fewer excuses.

**Key engineering choices:**
- Triple-wall vacuum insulation with copper lining for maximum thermal retention
- Wide mouth (accepts ice cubes and shaker-style powders) with a narrower sip spout
- Weight-conscious build (24 oz is about 13 oz empty) for runners and cyclists
- Optional carry loop and bike-cage-compatible diameter

**Ad placement strategy:** Retargeting banners on fitness blogs and running apps, short-form video on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube pre-roll, sponsored posts in athlete communities, and Google Display placements on gear-review and race-calendar sites.
