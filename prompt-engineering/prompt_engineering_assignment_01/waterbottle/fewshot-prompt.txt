--------------
--  Prompt  --
--------------

Write a product description for a water bottle.

Make it sound similar to these brief examples, but don't copy them verbatim, as these are not real products.

1.
Neon Pink Water Bottle

Reusable bottle with a distinct neon pink coating on the outside.
This makes it easy to spot, even in the wilderness.

Comes with a neon pink straw embedded into the lid as well.

2.
Aluminum food-safe bottle

Lightweight and durable due to the aluminum core,
with a food-safe plastic shell for safe drinking.

--------------
-- Response --
--------------

Matte Black Insulated Water Bottle

Reusable water bottle with a sleek matte black exterior and double-wall insulated construction.

Designed to keep drinks cold for hours while preventing condensation on the outside.

Features a secure screw-on lid to help prevent leaks when carried in a bag.

--------------
-- Analysis --
--------------

As instructed, the LLM did not copy verbatim, instead including new details.
Also, the response is short and succinct, just like the examples.

However, the response is lacking in terms of the creativity front,
which was desired as indicated by the second half of the instructions.

Possibly more examples could help, or even feeding this prompt several times
and using it as input to another prompt after.
