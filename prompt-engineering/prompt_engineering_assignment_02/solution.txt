Judgment calls I made:

Sarcasm counts as negative. Examples are R022, R024, R030, R066, R068, R078, R080, R085, R098 and R108. They read as praise on the surface but are complaints.
Mixed means the review has both a real positive and a real negative. This includes “good product, but problem X”, “bad product, but great support”, and lukewarm “good for the price” reviews.
Neutral covers questions (e.g. “Does this work with a PS5?”), flat statements (“It’s a blanket”), and reviews written before the product was used (e.g. “Haven’t opened them yet”).
Shipping-only complaints (R057, R062, R093) are counted as negative, even though they say nothing about the product itself.

The borderline reviews could reasonably go either way, so the counts could shift by a few. I can also break this down by product or add the list of review IDs per category.
