# Prompt Log: Water Bottle

**Model used: ChatGPT**

## Part 1 Version 1:
**Simple Prompt:**

```text
Write a product description for a water bottle.
```

**Typical output:**

> **Stay Hydrated, Stay Refreshed! 💧**
> 
> Stay refreshed throughout the day with our sleek and durable water bottle. Designed for everyday convenience, it's perfect for work, school, the gym, or outdoor adventures. Its lightweight, reusable design makes staying hydrated easy while helping reduce single-use plastic waste.
> 
> **Key Features:**
> - **Durable Design:** Built for everyday use.
> - **Lightweight & Portable:** Easy to carry wherever you go.
> - **Reusable & Eco-Friendly:** Helps reduce disposable plastic waste.
> - **Modern & Stylish:** A sleek design that fits your lifestyle.
> - **Easy to Clean:** Simple to maintain for daily use.
> 
> Whether you're heading to the gym or tackling a busy day, this water bottle is the perfect companion to keep you hydrated.
