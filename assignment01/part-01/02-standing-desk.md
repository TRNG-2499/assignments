# Prompt Log: Standing Desk

## Part 1 Version 1:
**Simple Prompt:**

```text
Write a product description for a Standing Desk.
```

**Typical output:**

> # Modern Adjustable Standing Desk – Comfort Meets Productivity
>
> Upgrade your workspace with our **Modern Adjustable Standing Desk**, designed to bring comfort, style, and flexibility to your daily routine. Whether you're working from home, studying, or gaming, this desk makes it easy to switch between sitting and standing throughout the day.
>
> Featuring a spacious desktop, sturdy construction, and a sleek, modern design, it fits perfectly into any home office or professional workspace.
>
> ## Key Features
>
> - **Adjustable Height:** Easily switch between sitting and standing positions for a more comfortable work experience.
> - **Spacious Desktop:** Plenty of room for monitors, laptops, keyboards, and other essentials.
> - **Sturdy & Durable:** Built for stability and reliable everyday use.
> - **Modern Design:** A clean, minimalist look that complements any workspace.
> - **Versatile Use:** Ideal for home offices, gaming setups, studying, and professional environments.
>
> ## Work Smarter. Stay Comfortable.
>
> Transform your workspace with a standing desk that combines functionality, comfort, and modern style. Whether you're tackling a busy workday or enjoying your favorite hobbies, this desk adapts to your needs.


## Part 2 Version 2:
**Improved Prompt (role + context + constraints):**

```text
You are a sales representative at Amazon with a specialty in selling high-end standing desks.

Write a product description for the standing desks that will be hung on a wall. Key Features:
- a BIFMA-compliant height range of 48.7
- a patent-pending FlexMount Cable Manager
- enhanced stability

Audience: Jeff Bezos looking to reduce physical strain with the freedom to easily switch between sitting and standing throughout his day.

Rules:
- You have to make Mr.Bezos buy the product or you will be fired
- Maintain a calm, minimalist tone and avoid any aggressive or pushy sales pitch
- Do not use overused marketing buzzwords such as game changer, revolutionary, ultimate, seamless, or sleek
```

**Output:**
> ## Elevate Your Workspace. Move with Confidence.
>
> Designed for those who value comfort, precision, and simplicity, our premium standing desk brings thoughtful engineering to every workday.
>
> Whether you're leading meetings, reviewing decisions, or focusing on your next big idea, your workspace should adapt to you—not the other way around.
>
> ### Key Features
>
> - **Ergonomic Height Adjustment:** A BIFMA-compliant height range reaching 48.7 inches allows you to transition comfortably between sitting and standing, helping reduce physical strain throughout the day.
> - **Patent-Pending FlexMount Cable Manager:** Keep cables organized and out of sight, creating a clean, distraction-free workspace.
> - **Enhanced Stability:** Built with a reinforced structure to minimize movement, providing a steady surface whether you're sitting or standing.
>
> ### A Workspace That Moves With You.
>
> Mr. Bezos, your time is valuable, and your comfort should never be an afterthought. This desk gives you the freedom to change positions throughout the day without interrupting your focus.
>
> Because the best workspace isn't one you have to think about. It's one that simply works for you.
