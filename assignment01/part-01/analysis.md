# Reflection

**Analysis** – A brief reflection (a few short paragraphs or bullet points) covering:
   - Which techniques you used, and where in your prompts you used them
   - How each technique affected the output. Did it improve, stay the same, or get worse? Point to specific differences between versions.
   - Any times the model ignored part of your prompt or made up details you didn't provide, and what you changed in response
   - Which technique made the biggest difference for you, and why you think that was

**Response:**
Throughout this assignment, I found this very educational and interesting how different prompts would result in different answers. The techniques I used were mostly role prompting, giving context, and using constraints. Each technique has definitely impacted each result. In the simple prompt, the LLM would give a very generic response in the product such as a catchy headline following up with introduction paragraph. All of the descriptions would have a key feature then a closing paragraph following up with a marketing statement. Sometimes the model would ignore part of my instructions I have provided. For example the noise cancelling headphones, the model didn't follow the instructions of having two paragraphs and would only end with one paragraph.
I think setting the role and rules definitely made the biggest difference for me. For example, if I ask the model to act like a Yelp reviewer or someone pitching a product on Shark Tank, the responses are very different from using a simple prompt. I think this is because assigning a role gives the model a better understanding of the tone, style, and perspective I want it to use. Setting rules also helps the model understand how I want the response to be structured and what information to include. Although setting rules doesn't guarantee the exact output I want, it still makes a noticeable difference in the quality and consistency of the responses.
